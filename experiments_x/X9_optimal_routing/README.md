## Paper objects tested (labels from `main_body.tex` / `appendix_core.tex`)
* **thm:optimal-routing (i)**: `Phi(lambda) = sum_x pi(x) max_u [v(x,u) - lambda c(x,u)]` is convex and strictly decreasing with unique root `eta* = max_g eta(g)`; a router is efficiency-optimal iff in every state it uses only maximizers of `v - eta* c`; for {direct, attempt-then-escalate} this is `p(x) W_{eta*}(x) >= eta* a(x)` (prop:threshold, eq:margin; exact matched-reference form `W = lambda cbar_h + v_h - vbar_h`, app:matched-reference).
* **thm:optimal-routing (ii)**: Dinkelbach iteration `lambda_0 = eta_B`, `lambda_{t+1} = eta(g_t)` is monotone and finite; `Phi(eta_B) = max_g [Q_g + eta_B S_g]` (thm:surplus).
* **thm:optimal-routing (iii)**: under `E[V] >= vbar - eps` an optimal router randomizes in at most one state (Charnes-Cooper LP eq:app-cc-lp), a multiplier `kappa >= 0` makes every optimal router a state-wise maximizer of `(1+kappa) v - eta*_eps c`; the two-action price is `eta*_eps/(1+kappa)`.
* **thm:surplus** accounting for every policy realized: `eta - eta_B = (Q + eta_B S)/(cbar - S)`, net gain `E[V - eta_B D]` = accepted quality change `E[H(Y_P - Y_B)]` + `eta_B` (avoided reference work `E[H C_B]` - attempt cost `E[A]` - recovery cost 0).

## Setup
**Data.** `$ACROUTER_DATA/coderouterbench/id_results_long.csv`: 9,999 tasks x 8 models, `score` in [0,1], token-price `cost_usd`, `latency_ms`. Splits: probing 7,080 (train+val; all primitives fitted here) / id_test 2,919 (test; realizations only, never fitted on). Cross-check: always-Opus id_test total $34.02 (paper $34.02), mean score 0.4383 (paper 43.83). 148 Qwen3-Max rows have no token record (cost 0, score 0; kept as recorded). Zero-latency rows by model: {'glm-5': 1452, 'qwen3.5-plus': 1070, 'MiniMax-M2.7': 541, 'kimi-k2.5': 84, 'Qwen3-Max': 2, 'claude-opus-4-6': 1} (71% of them have score 0; kept; they affect the seconds basis only).

**States.** (i) 21 dimension x source cells (source = second underscore token of `task_id`); (ii) 9 dimensions. `pi(x)` = cell frequency; the rule uses the probing frequencies (all primitives come from probing), the realization uses the id_test tasks themselves (id_test frequencies). Both frequency vectors are in `results.json:states` and in the per-state table below; `eta*` recomputed with id_test frequencies and probing `v, c` differs by usd/cell21: +2.42%, usd/dim9: +1.66%, sec/cell21: +4.77%, sec/dim9: +3.89%.

**Menu U(x)** (22 actions, identical in every state): ex-ante direct dispatch to each of the 8 models (value = mean score, cost = mean `cost_usd` of that model in the cell on probing), and attempt-then-escalate for each of the 7 non-Opus primaries m with the oracle verifier `H = 1{score_m >= theta}`, theta in {1, 0.5}, fallback claude-opus-4-6: per task `V = H Y_m + (1-H) Y_Opus`, `D = C_m + (1-H) C_Opus` (dollars) or `lat_m + (1-H) lat_Opus` (seconds, sequential). **Reference policy** = always-Opus = action `direct:claude-opus-4-6`, so it is admissible (`eta(g_B) = eta_B` exactly). Design-document deviation: the X8 judge-theta grid is not part of this menu (X8 is a separate live experiment; the task protocol fixes the 22-action menu).

**Resources.** Everything is run twice: dollars (csv `cost_usd`) and seconds (sequential: `latency_ms` of the primary plus, on a miss, of Opus). `eta = sum V / sum D` (ratio of sums) everywhere; `eta_B` (probing) = 34.727 per dollar, 0.05478 per second; id_test 37.609 per dollar, 0.05926 per second.

**Computation.** `Phi` on a 401-point grid; root by bisection to relative width 1e-13; Dinkelbach from `lambda_0 = eta_B` with deterministic argmax policies; constrained problems as the Charnes-Cooper LP (`scipy.optimize.linprog`, `method='highs-ds'`), `kappa` = dual multiplier of the floor row (checked against the analytic tie in the randomizing state and against the root of `Phi_eps`, computed independently by a fixed-price floor LP plus bisection). Independent checks of the root: an LP without floor (exact maximum over all randomized routers), exhaustive enumeration of all 2^|X| deterministic policies for each of the 14 two-action sub-menus, 2,000 coordinate-ascent starts and 200,000 random deterministic policies on the full menu, and a one-swap test of the argmax policy.

**Baselines on id_test.** always-Opus; DimensionBest (`$ACR/id/voter_decisions/dimension_best.jsonl`, 3251 rows, 2919 on id_test; ex-ante dispatch paying only the chosen model); ACRouter's released decisions (`outputs/current/id_decisions.jsonl`, 2919 rows; shares {'claude-opus-4-6': 1788, 'gpt-5.4': 533, 'Qwen3-Max': 318, 'kimi-k2.5': 280}; scored as ex-ante dispatch of `chosen_model`, without router/verifier overhead); the best fixed cascade (7 primaries x 2 theta, selected on probing) and, as the one-state version of the rule, the best single direct dispatch (selected on probing); the per-task oracle (state = task; Dinkelbach and Charnes-Cooper LP on id_test itself; an in-sample upper bound for any state-based policy with this menu).

**Statistics.** Paired bootstrap: the same 1000 multinomial task resamples of id_test are applied to every policy, differences are taken per resample, 95% percentile CIs. Floor satisfaction rate = fraction of resamples with `E[V] >= vbar - eps`, `vbar` from the same resample. Fit uncertainty: 1000 multinomial resamples of probing refit `eta*`, `eta*_eps`, `kappa`, the number of randomizing states and the per-state actions. Claims are labelled IDENTITY / IN-SAMPLE / OUT-OF-SAMPLE.

## Limitations
* The verifier is the oracle `H = 1{score_m >= theta}`: attempt actions are upper bounds for real verifiers; the X8 judge/sandbox operating points are not in this menu.
* Efficiency `eta` without a floor rewards cheap dispatch: in dollars the unconstrained optimum sends every cell to Qwen3-Max or kimi-k2.5 and gives up 0.016 of the reference's mean score (0.020 realized). The floor is what makes the rule quality-safe, and its in-sample version needs a safety margin out of sample (C5, `oos_safety_margin_for_95pct`).
* ACRouter is scored from its released per-task decisions as ex-ante dispatch; its router, sandbox-verifier and memory costs and its actual escalation traces are not included (X7 covers the live accounting). DimensionBest and the fixed cascades are exactly reproducible from the matrix.
* The per-task oracle is optimized on id_test itself (an in-sample upper bound); its bootstrap CI re-optimizes per resample and is reported separately from the fixed-assignment CI.
* Seconds use recorded latencies with zero-latency rows kept; sequential accounting only (no concurrent fallback launch; X5 treats hedging).
* Fit-uncertainty CIs (probing resamples) and realization CIs (id_test resamples) are reported separately, not combined.
